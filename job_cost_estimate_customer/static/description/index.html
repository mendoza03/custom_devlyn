Job Sales Estimation Module: Materials, Labour & Overhead Breakdown

This module enables the creation of detailed job estimates by itemizing costs across materials, labour, and overhead. It allows users to generate accurate cost breakdowns and seamlessly send estimates to customers. Additionally, the module provides functionality to convert job estimates into official sales quotations, streamlining the sales workflow and ensuring customers receive precise and comprehensive pricing information.

Key Features of Job Estimate Module

  • Create Job Sales Estimates:
    • Enables salespersons and sales managers to create detailed job sales estimates for customers, including itemized breakdowns of material, labour, and overhead costs.
  • Generate Sales Quotations from Estimates:
    • Users can directly convert a job estimate into a sales quotation from the estimate form view. The quotation will automatically reference the related job estimate.
  • Email Job Estimates to Customers:
    • Facilitates efficient communication by allowing users to print and send job estimates via email directly to customers.
  • Job Type Configuration:
    • Allows project managers to define and manage job types used in job estimates, enabling better categorization and reporting.
  • Visual and Video Documentation:
    • Refer to the included screenshots and demonstration video for a detailed overview of the modules features and usage.

Top Highlights of Job Estimation Features

  • Detailed Job Estimation:
    • Build comprehensive job estimates by breaking down material, labour, and overhead costs.
  • Customizable Cost Structure:
    • Tailor job estimates with specific cost inputs to match individual project requirements.
  • Customer Communication:
    • Easily send finalized job estimates to customers for review and approval.
  • Quotation Conversion:
    • Streamline the sales process by converting job estimates into formal sales quotations with a single click.
  • PDF Report Generation:
    • Export job estimates as PDF documents for sharing, printing, or internal records.
